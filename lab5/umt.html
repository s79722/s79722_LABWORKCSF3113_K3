About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT), formerly known as Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM), is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It began as a Fisheries and Marine Science Centre of Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM) in 1979 before gaining full university status in February 2007.

Positioned along the South China Sea coastline, UMT is widely recognised as Malaysia's premier marine science and aquatic resource university. Its main campus in Gong Badak serves as a hub for research in oceanography, maritime management, environmental sustainability, and coastal community development.

Today, UMT offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programmes across science, technology, fisheries, maritime studies, and business. Through its specialized research institutes and training vessels, the university actively contributes to national and global marine conservation efforts.
